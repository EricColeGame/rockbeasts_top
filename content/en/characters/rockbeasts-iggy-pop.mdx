### Build a Buffer Before You Chase Prestige

One of the most common mistakes in band-management games is chasing the biggest opportunity on the board while your band is still fragile. In Rockbeasts, the temptation is to treat a legendary figure's attention as the main quest. It is not. It is a multiplier on whatever state your band is already in — and multipliers on chaos just produce more chaos.

Practical steps:

1. Stabilize relationships between band members before pursuing high-profile story content.
2. Keep a cash reserve that covers at least a few weeks of expenses.
3. Rotate who gets the spotlight so resentment does not pool in one place.
4. Read dialogue carefully; tone often signals consequences better than text does.

## Managing Expectations and Your Band

A final word of caution: do not let a single character become your entire strategy. The Rockbeasts Iggy Pop storyline is memorable, but the game's core loop is still about the five people in the van. Handle the van first.

Quick checklist before you chase any legendary storyline:

- Is every band member's morale above the danger zone?
- Do you have enough money to survive a bad month?
- Have you resolved the last argument, or just postponed it?
- Are you choosing the honest option or the flattering one?

Answer those honestly and the mentor scenes will hit harder — because you will actually have something worth being judged on.
